# Gameplay Loop

## Spawn

## Search for Beacon

Camp locations are marked on the map and each contains a beacon pointing to the objective.

## Follow Beacon / Hunt

The beacon points to the Chipsite and can be followed to find where to perform the extraction. Beacons are found at camps and are worth some ammount of experience or currency if extracted.

## Extract Tech

Right now the tech is a strong weapon with limited charge. Possibly able to be partially recharged at camps. Additional tech can be found at camps, just small pieces worth less than the objective. The extraction is an involved process, 35 seconds of essentially one team member being out of the fight.

## Return to Spawn or Another Extraction Point
